PLASTIC

Rinse before recycling

Empty and rinse plastic bottles and containers before putting them into recycling.

DO Empty → Rinse → Dry → Recycle
📦
PAPER

Keep paper clean

Clean cardboard and paper can often be recycled. Heavily food-soiled paper may need another disposal method.

TIP Flatten cardboard boxes before recycling.
🍾
GLASS

Handle glass carefully

Keep glass separate from other waste and handle broken pieces carefully.

TIP Check your local recycling rules for glass.
🔩
METAL

Don't waste your cans

Aluminium and steel cans can often be recycled. Empty and rinse them before disposal.

DO Empty → Rinse → Recycle
💻
E-WASTE

Never throw electronics in regular trash

Phones, laptops, batteries and other electronics should be taken to appropriate e-waste collection points.

DON'T Put batteries or electronics in household trash.
🍎
FOOD WASTE

Reduce food waste

Plan meals, store food properly and use leftovers before buying more.

TIP Buy what you need and use what you buy.
🌿
COMPOST

Turn food scraps into something useful

Many fruit and vegetable scraps can be composted instead of being sent to landfill.

TRY Fruit peels → Vegetable scraps → Compost
🛍️
PLASTIC

Carry reusable bags

Reusable bags can help reduce your dependence on single-use plastic bags.

SWAP Single-use bag → Reusable bag
